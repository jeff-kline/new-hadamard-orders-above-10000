## Hits to record

**Hit A (closest to Theorems C/D), not anticipating.** vatskishan03/Hadamard `search/dcs/README.md`: "A DCS of length g free of +-is, Golay lengths g1 and g2 and their Lemmas 1-3 and Theorem 7 give a Hadamard matrix of order 8(g1 g + g2) (their Corollary 9)." Link: https://github.com/vatskishan03/Hadamard/blob/main/search/dcs/README.md. This is the single-DCS Kamali-Kharaghani chain with a real Golay pair, order 8(g1 g + g2); it does not use two dihedral pairs on independent tensor factors or the 16(m+n) formula. Same repo's hadamard/12472 and /5912 READMEs say the 8(g1 g + g2) chain "does not reach" those orders. Last commit 2026-11-06.

Link: https://github.com/vatskishan03/Hadamard/blob/main/complex-hadamard/CONFERENCE.md. This is the plain (non-powered) Yamada-Kiyasu template plus Turyn doubling, and is already credited in the note.
